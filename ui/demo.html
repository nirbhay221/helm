The bulwark

Nobody games the payout, on either side.

Sybil and collusion defence

A cheater spins up fake identities to claim a bigger slice of a shared reward pool. A naive even split rewards the fraud. Helm detects the cluster, merges it, and the cheater's take collapses to a single honest share.

Marketplace abuse, buyers and sellers

Fault follows the side the returns spread across: many sellers hit means the buyer is at fault; many buyers hit means the seller is. A pattern, not a single dispute.

Keep it in, not just cash it out

Creators can pool their payout and own a stake.

Instead of taking the money and leaving, a creator can leave it in a shared pool that tracks a basket of real assets: US stocks, real estate, and restaurant chains. Each creator owns units proportional to what they put in. Priced on real market data.

How the money actually moves

Every payment clears a verifier bank, then settles on PayPal.

The agent never pays directly. It proposes a batch; a bank of checks must pass first; then one atomic Payouts call settles it, idempotent on a unique batch id so a retry never double pays. Money in equals money out plus fees, checked with plain arithmetic.
